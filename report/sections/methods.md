**Data.** A fixed holdout of 2,000,000<!-- src/uplift/data.py HOLDOUT_SIZE --> users is never used to fit, tune or choose features. Nested training samples of 100,000 to 5,000,000<!-- src/uplift/data.py TRAIN_SIZES --> users come from one shuffled pool. Visits are the main outcome, conversion secondary. A LightGBM response model, fitted on training rows only, cuts 12<!-- src/uplift/data.py FEATURES --> features to 10<!-- results/kept_features.json --> by importance. **Baseline.** A LightGBM model of the outcome that ignores treatment. **Learners.** The S-learner fits one model with treatment as input, the T-learner one model per group, and the X-learner imputes individual effects and blends two effect models by the treated share. One LightGBM setting per outcome, chosen from 6<!-- src/uplift/pipeline.py GRID --> candidates by 3<!-- src/uplift/pipeline.py tune_base folds --> fold cross validation on 300,000<!-- src/uplift/pipeline.py tune_base n_rows --> training rows, is shared by all but the X-learner effect models<!-- src/uplift/learners.py XLearner effect_params -->. **Evaluation.** The Qini coefficient is the area between a model's Qini curve (extra outcomes from treating the top ranked users, estimated from outcome rates) and the random line. A paired bootstrap of 200<!-- src/uplift/metrics.py bootstrap_metrics n_boot --> holdout resamples scores all models on the same rows. A one sided test compares each learner with the baseline, Bonferroni corrected over 3<!-- docs/decision_log.md 13 Nov --> comparisons per outcome. **Further analyses.** Uplift by decile, a data size curve, a policy simulation at 10, 20 and 30 percent budgets, a rank correlation with the baseline, a harm check, and a synthetic test with a known effect. Seeds are fixed. Code: github.com/neuralnomad26/causal-uplift.
