H1 is partly supported. On visits (5M training rows, 2M holdout), only the S-learner beats the response baseline (corrected p 0.015 for 3 tests; significant means below 0.05). The X-learner is borderline (corrected p 0.075, or 0.045 with another bootstrap seed) and the T-learner is not significant (0.33). Qini coefficient (Figure 1; gain over response, uncorrected 95 percent interval): response 0.00340, S 0.00356 (+0.000157, 0.000087 to 0.000232), T 0.00358 (+0.000177, \mbox{-0.000091} to 0.000457), X 0.00369 (+0.000285, 0.000016 to 0.000526). On conversion no learner beats the baseline. At a 10 percent budget (Figure 4) the X-learner adds 1,133 visits per 100,000 users contacted over the response baseline (paired interval 728 to 1,442, uncorrected), though its corrected test is borderline; at 20 and 30 percent every visit gain interval includes zero.
