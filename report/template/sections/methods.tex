\textbf{Data.} A fixed holdout of 2,000,000 users is never used to fit, tune or choose features. Nested training samples of 100,000 to 5,000,000 users come from one shuffled pool. Visits are the main outcome, conversion secondary. A LightGBM response model, fitted on training rows only, cuts 12 features to 10 by importance. \textbf{Baseline.} A LightGBM model of the outcome that ignores treatment. \textbf{Learners.} The S-learner fits one model with treatment as input, the T-learner one model per group, and the X-learner imputes individual effects and blends two effect models by the treated share. One LightGBM setting per outcome, chosen from 6 candidates by 3 fold cross validation on 300,000 training rows, is shared by all but the X-learner effect models. \textbf{Evaluation.} The Qini coefficient is the area between a model's Qini curve (extra outcomes from treating the top ranked users, estimated from outcome rates) and the random line. A paired bootstrap of 200 holdout resamples scores all models on the same rows. A one sided test compares each learner with the baseline, Bonferroni corrected over 3 comparisons per outcome. \textbf{Further analyses.} Uplift by decile, a data size curve, a policy simulation at 10, 20 and 30 percent budgets, a rank correlation with the baseline, a harm check, and a synthetic test with a known effect. Seeds are fixed. Code: github.com/neuralnomad26/causal-uplift.
