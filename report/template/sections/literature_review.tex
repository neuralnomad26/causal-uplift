Ascarza (2018) showed that targeting customers likely to leave can miss those whose behaviour an offer could change [1].

Gutierrez and Gérardy (2017) explain how uplift modelling estimates an intervention's effect instead of just predicting responses [2].

Radcliffe (2007) introduced the Qini curve to evaluate how well uplift models rank customers [3].

Künzel et al. (2019) presented the S, T and X learners for estimating treatment effects [4].

Diemert et al. (2018) provided the Criteo dataset for uplift modelling research [5].

Our project compares response targeting with these learners using Criteo data, a fixed holdout and different training sample sizes.
