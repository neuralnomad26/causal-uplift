Who actually benefits from an offer, not who is likely to respond, and why that matters for a limited budget. Criteo Uplift v2.1, 13,979,592 users from a randomized advertising experiment, 85 percent treated and 15 percent control, 12 anonymized features, two outcomes. On the 1M sample across the ten kept features, the largest standardized difference is 0.0536, below the 0.1 threshold. A fixed holdout of 2,000,000 users is never used for fitting, tuning or feature selection. Training samples from 100,000 to 5,000,000 users are nested. We keep 10 of 12 features, dropping the two least important. The file is ordered, not shuffled, so we always sample at random with a fixed seed.
