On visits the gains over the baseline are small, and only the S-learner's gain is significant (corrected p 0.015). The X-learner is borderline and depends on the bootstrap seed. The T-learner is not significant. The S-learner ranks users almost like the baseline (Spearman 0.96), which is consistent with small gains, but the data cannot show whether uplift grows with response. The gains are at the smallest budget: at 10 percent, for example, the X-learner adds 1,133 visits per 100,000 contacted (interval 728 to 1,442), while at 20 and 30 percent every interval includes zero. On conversion no learner beats the baseline. Users predicted to be harmed show no clear harm, but intervals are wide. A synthetic test shows the T-learner recovers a known effect, which supports the evaluation code.
