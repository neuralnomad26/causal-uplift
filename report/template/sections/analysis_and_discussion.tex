On visits, gains over the baseline are small and only the \mbox{S-learner}'s is significant (corrected p~0.015). The \mbox{X-learner} is borderline and depends on the bootstrap seed, and the \mbox{T-learner} is not significant. The \mbox{S-learner} ranks users almost like the baseline (Spearman 0.96), but this does not show that uplift grows in proportion to response. Gains concentrate at the smallest budget: at 10 percent the \mbox{X-learner} adds 1,133 visits per 100,000 contacted over the baseline (interval 728 to 1,442), while at 20 and 30 percent every interval includes zero. On conversion no learner beats the baseline. Checks: predicted harm groups show no clear harm (wide intervals); a synthetic test recovers a known effect (\mbox{T-learner}). Limitations: intervals resample only the holdout, and S and X were not retrained across seeds.
