Does uplift targeting beat response targeting (H1)? We test S, T and X learners on 2,000,000 holdout Criteo users. H1 is partly supported: only the \mbox{S-learner} is significantly better on visits (corrected p~0.015). At a 10 percent budget the \mbox{X-learner} adds 1,133 visits per 100,000 contacted over the baseline, but its test is borderline. Conversion shows no gain. Treatment was not perfectly random, so gains may be overstated.
