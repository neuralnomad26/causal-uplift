Does uplift targeting beat targeting by likely response (H1)? We test S, T and X uplift learners on 2,000,000 held out Criteo users. H1 is partly supported: only the S-learner is significantly better on visits (corrected p 0.015). At a 10 percent budget the X-learner adds 1,133 visits per 100,000 contacted, but its test is borderline. Conversion shows no gain. Treatment was not perfectly random, so gains may be overstated.
